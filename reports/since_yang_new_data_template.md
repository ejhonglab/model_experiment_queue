From 2026-11-10 on, further material of hers was added in steps: the traces and the noise scale of every KC of the panel recordings, calcium imaging of APL itself in the calyx, recordings of APL and the KC claws together in two colours, tables derived from connectomes in separate work, and her answers on how the recordings were made and how the target should be read. Each KC keeps its number of claws and each glomerulus its number of claws; which KC gets which glomerulus changes. APL effect: Of one odor pair, its odor-odor distance with APL on minus its distance with APL off. In the KC data, the control flies' distance minus the distance with APL output blocked (TNT), each the mean over recordings of the cosine distance; in a model, its distance with APL on minus its distance with APL acutely off.
APL-effect loss: The root mean squared difference between a model's and the KC data's per-pair APL effect over the 120 odor pairs; lower is better. It splits exactly into a mean part and a pattern part, and its floor is the loss that a second sample of the same flies would show against the first.
pattern part: The APL-effect loss with both means removed: how far the model's pattern of the APL effect across the 120 pairs is from the flies'. It splits exactly into an amplitude part (the model spreads its effect over the pairs more or less widely than the flies) and a shape part.
calibrated readout: Each model spike count n replaced by n / (n + k) before the distances are taken, with k set on the model's counts with APL on. It compresses large counts as a calcium indicator does and leaves which KCs respond unchanged.
realism bands: The four ranges that a model has to stay inside for its loss to count: its response rate with APL on, the ratio of its response rates with APL off and on, its response rate ρ with APL on, and its distribution of the number of odors each KC responds to, each set from the recordings.
baseline-noise units: A KC's response divided by the standard deviation of its own signal within the pre-odor windows of its recording. Unlike the recordings' z-scored unit, it does not grow with how much the KC responds.
best family: The family of models that stands best on the main target: APL inhibition weighted by how broadly each KC is driven, KC–KC inhibition, no per-claw threshold and no glomerulus gain, fitted to the APL effect at the control flies' response rate. common error: The part of the best family's pattern error that every wiring shares: its APL effect is too small among 2h, IaA, pa, eb, ep and ms and too large among 1-8ol, 1-5ol, 1-6ol and 6al.
calyx: The part of the mushroom body in which the KCs' dendrites, their claws, receive the PN boutons, and which APL innervates throughout. The recordings of APL image one plane through it.
read in proportion: A model's APL ratio taken as it is, as if the indicator reported APL's activity in proportion to it.
indicator family: The indicator nonlinearities through which a model's APL response is read before a ratio is taken, from one in proportion to one about as steep as the reported cooperativity of the indicator.
leak: In the dual-colour recordings, the part of APL's green signal that appears in the red channel of the KCs, with APL's own pattern. Its size is the slope of the red channel on the green one in flies in which only APL carries an indicator.
vial series: Yang's recordings of APL of October and November 2021, made as a control: every recording holds ms, paa and pa at three concentrations each and two further vials of pa or of paa at the same concentrations, so that one odor is delivered from three vials.
settled level: A model stage's level before the odor taken over the last quarter of a second before it, by which time the KCs and APL, which the simulation starts from zero half a second before the odor, hold the level that they have without an odor.
raw only: Of a recording: only the raw movie exists, with none of Yang's processed products. By a rule of 2026-11-15 such recordings are not read, processed or analysed until further notice, since she is likely to have judged them of lower quality than those that she processed.

quality caveat: Yang has released these recordings for use and has not checked their quality. No recording was left out on her word, and every result that rests on them carries this.

## In brief {#brief}

- **The recordings' unit is relative to each KC.** One z is {{data:kc_scale_ratio_0 ~ .1f}} times the baseline noise in a KC that responds to none of the 16 odors and {{data:kc_scale_ratio_11plus ~ .1f}} times in one that responds to eleven or more. Since 2026-11-10 the realism bands therefore count a response of the flies as a three-trial mean above {{data:noise_threshold ~ .2f}} times the KC's baseline noise ([the unit](#unit)).
- **In that fixed unit the flies' mean APL effect is larger and its pattern is the same:** {{data:noise_apl_effect_mean ~ +.3f}} against {{data:apl_effect_mean ~ +.3f}} in the recordings' own unit, with ρ={{data:noise_apl_effect_rho_vs_z ~ .2f}} on the per-pair APL effect between the two units. Which unit the target takes is a choice still to be made ([the data side of the main target](#target-unit)).
- **The APL effect is made at the odor's onset, and the indicator is slow.** The effect is largest during the pulse and falls through the response. A kernel fitted to the control flies' traces rises over {{E84/kernel_fits.csv:level=subset;subset=control flies;selection=resp;fit=free;drive=onset:rise ~ .1f}} s and decays over {{E84/kernel_fits.csv:level=subset;subset=control flies;selection=resp;fit=free;drive=onset:decay ~ .0f}} s, so that a model read as the flies were recorded reads what its spike counts at the flies' 2 s pulse read ([the time course](#time-course)).
- **APL's activity in the calyx is mostly one pattern that all odors share,** {{E79/E79_model_vs_measured.csv:blur_px=2.0;mode=means kept;column=share_first_sv:measured_all_median ~ .0%}} to {{E79/E79_model_vs_measured.csv:blur_px=20.0;mode=means kept;column=share_first_sv:measured_all_median ~ .0%}} of a recording's summed squares, and the smaller part that is a stimulus's own is the odor's ([where APL responds](#maps-where), [the vial series](#vial-series)).
- **Along concentration the best family's APL is flatter than the flies' APL.** From paa at 10^-4 to paa at 10^-5 the flies' APL falls to {{E98/a3_ratios.csv:ratio_id=V2;reading=order_matched;subset=all:ratio ~ .2f}} of its response ({{E98/a3_ratios.csv:ratio_id=V2;reading=order_matched;subset=all:lo90 ~ .2f}} to {{E98/a3_ratios.csv:ratio_id=V2;reading=order_matched;subset=all:hi90 ~ .2f}}) and the family's to {{E98/comparison_table.csv:comparison=V2, paa at 10^-5 from validation2/diag:model_linear_min ~ .2f}} to {{E98/comparison_table.csv:comparison=V2, paa at 10^-5 from validation2/diag:model_linear_max ~ .2f}} read in proportion, on all eight wirings, with the concentrations rising or shuffled, and in the same direction in every other set of flies ([the picture](#picture-concentration)).
- **That flatness is made before APL.** In the family APL follows the summed input to its claws, the claws follow the PNs, and the model's antennal lobe evens out the odors' totals. A weaker normalization there brings APL's fall inside the flies' interval through the indicator family, and at fixed scales it costs on the main target: at the step that the flies' APL picks, the pattern part of the mean over eight wirings goes from {{E102/b_readings.csv:unit=all8;kind=population;readout=cal;step=identity:z8_pattern ~ .3f}} to {{E102/b_readings.csv:unit=all8;kind=population;readout=cal;step=inhadd1000:z8_pattern ~ .3f}} (lower is better), and the KCs' responses to the weakest odors move further from the flies'. The family fitted again at that step is in hand ([where the flatness is made](#picture-before-apl)).
- **Across odors near 10^-3 the flies' APL does not constrain the family.** Three sets of flies disagree about ms, and two vials of one odor differ persistently by a factor of {{lit:1.3764 # two to the power of the persistent spread, results/E103_apl_across_odors_heldout/d_summary.json: y1, carrying, all, persistent_sd (0.46092) ~ .2f}} per standard deviation, so that a ratio between two odors read from one pair of vials is known to no better than a factor of about one and a half ([the vial series](#vial-series)).
- **The dual-colour recordings decided none of their three questions.** The channels are as they had been inferred and the leak of APL's signal into the KC channel is small, {{lit:0.028261733324614166 # results/E101_apl_vs_kc_claws/b_leak.json: b_G ~ .3f}} of it, but its size does not carry from one control fly to the other; Yang's images of each channel's baseline would settle it ([the dual-colour recordings](#dual-colour)).

## The traces and the noise scales of the KC recordings {#kc-traces}

**What the data are.** Every value of the KC data had been one number per KC and trial: the mean of the KC's signal from 2 to 8 s after the onset of a 2 s odor pulse, minus its mean over the 5 s before. From Yang's processing outputs for the same recordings two things were added: for every KC its baseline noise, the standard deviation of its signal within the 51 pre-odor windows of its recording, beside the standard deviation of its whole trace; and the trace itself around each presentation. The stored values come back from them to the rounding of the stored numbers, and no model was simulated for any of what follows.

### The unit of the recordings, and the criterion of the realism bands {#unit}

**The recordings' unit grows with how much a KC responds.** The per-KC normalization of the recordings found, from the stored values alone, that each KC's trace is z-scored over its whole recording, and Yang confirmed it. One z is then the standard deviation of a trace that holds the KC's own odor responses: its median is {{data:kc_scale_ratio_0 ~ .2f}} times the baseline noise in the KCs that respond to none of the 16 odors, {{data:kc_scale_ratio_3 ~ .2f}} times in those that respond to three, {{data:kc_scale_ratio_8 ~ .2f}} to eight and {{data:kc_scale_ratio_11plus ~ .2f}} to eleven or more. The recordings' criterion for a response, a three-trial mean above 1 z, is therefore stricter for a KC that responds to much than for one that responds to little, where a model's criterion, two or more spikes to the odor, is the same for every KC.

**Since 2026-11-10 the realism bands count the flies' responses in baseline-noise units.** One threshold serves every KC of every recording, set on the control recordings alone so that their response rate is what it is by the recordings' own criterion. The models keep their two-spike criterion, and each saved model was scored again from its saved responses. The overall response rates hardly move. What moves is how the responses are spread over the KCs (table), which is what the band on the number of odors per KC compares; the reference model, which had been outside that band with APL off by the recordings' criterion, is inside it by the present one.

### The time course of the APL effect in the traces {#time-course}

**The effect is there from the first second of the odor and is largest during the pulse.** The time course of the APL effect in the flies' traces took the per-pair APL effect in windows of the response. It does not build up in the window that the target is taken from: it falls through the response (table). ## Yang's answers {#answers}

- **The unit.** Each KC's trace is z-scored over its whole recording, and a response is the mean from 2 to 8 s after the odor's onset minus the mean over the 5 s before. This led to the baseline-noise units of the realism bands.
- **The indicators.** The recordings of APL used GCaMP6f; the KC recordings used a GCaMP6s confined to the nucleus, except in the dual-colour recordings, where the KCs carried the red indicator jRGECO1a. The slow nuclear indicator fits the kernel measured from the traces.

### The flies' side, the model's level before the odor, and what did not change {#picture-rest}

**The flies' side.** The realism bands now compare a fixed criterion with a fixed criterion. The main target is unchanged, and two choices about it are explicit: its unit, on which the mean of the flies' effect depends and its pattern does not, and whether all eight recordings with APL output blocked (TNT) make it or the six that are alike. The effect is made at the odor's onset and is in every window of the response, and so is the common error.

**The model's level before the odor.** The first run of the comparison of strengths failed a check that a blank evokes nothing in the model's APL. The simulation starts the KCs, their claws and APL from zero half a second before the odor, and a mean over that half second reads APL low and the claws high. Every reading of APL on this page is therefore against a blank in the same run, with the settled level as its partner (against a blank, {{E98/a4_profile_carrying.csv:wiring=P3:a0_share ~ .0%}} to {{E98/a4_profile_carrying.csv:wiring=real:a0_share ~ .0%}} of APL's activity during an odor is there without one). The stage comparisons that had used the earlier level were read again: no spike count, response rate, realism band or number of the main target moved, and no verdict changed, while several readings of the claws did.

**What did not change.** No base model, bound or reference family changed because of any of the new data, and no number of the main target moved: the best family's pattern part as a mean over eight wirings stands at {{E72/population.csv:group=all:cal_pattern ~ .3f}} through the calibrated readout against a floor of {{E66/data_floor_parts.csv:data=graded;stat=apl_effect_rmse_centered:mean ~ .3f}} (lower is better), and all eight fits are inside the four realism bands. What the readings of APL add is a description of the family beside the bands: its APL is more even along concentration than the flies' APL and more even than the family's own KCs.
