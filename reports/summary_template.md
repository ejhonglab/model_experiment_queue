most atypical fly: Of the flies of one stage and panel, the fly that is furthest from the mean of the other flies, in one-fly units. A model is called inside at a stage if it is no further from the flies' mean than this fly is from the others.

See the section on what carries over from the APL16 work.

Each KC keeps its number of claws and each glomerulus its number of claws. A model on a re-drawn wiring stands for another fly with the same statistics.
APL16 panel: The 16 odors of the KC recordings made with APL on and with APL off: control flies, and flies with APL output blocked (TNT). Twelve of its odors are megamat odors. The best family was fitted to these recordings, and they are held out from every fit to the megamat stages.
realism bands: Four ranges that a model run on the APL16 panel has to stay inside: its response rate with APL on, the ratio of its response rates with APL off and with APL on, its response rate ρ with APL on, and its distribution of the number of odors per KC. They are reported for every model and never optimized. See the footnote on the bands.
calibrated readout: A saturating function of a model KC's spike count n, n / (n + k), applied before the model's distances between odors are taken. The half-saturation count k is set for each model on its own spike counts with APL on, and never on the APL effect. The APL-effect statistics on this page are read through it.
baseline-noise units: A response divided by the unit's own noise before the odor, the standard deviation of its signal within the pre-odor frames. The boutons, the claws and the KC somata are counted in these units.
pattern part: The part of the APL-effect loss that is left once the model's and the flies' mean effects are removed: the root mean squared difference of the two per-pair effects about their means. It is read with its amplitude part and its shape part. Lower is better.
APL-effect loss: The root mean squared difference, over the 120 odor pairs of the APL16 panel, between the model's and the flies' per-pair APL effect: the distance between two odors with APL on minus their distance with APL off. The main target of the earlier work, now a test on recordings that a fit to the stages never sees. Lower is better.

## What is recorded: the stages, the panels and the flies {#recordings}

This page follows one target. A model of the fly's olfactory pathway is held to the responses that were recorded at every stage of that pathway: the olfactory receptor neurons (ORNs), the projection neurons (PNs) at their dendrites and at their boutons, and the Kenyon cells (KCs) of the mushroom body at their claws and at their somata. The model and the flies are compared as populations, stage by stage.

The earlier work held models to what one inhibitory neuron of the mushroom body, APL, does to the distances between odors at the KCs. It is on the page "APL16 Summary". What carries over from it is [near the end of this page](#apl16-carryover).

### Three views of a population, and what the flies' views look like {#views}

**A population** is the table of trial-mean responses of all units of one stage to the odors of one panel. A bouton region has no glomerulus label, a claw region is not assigned to a KC, and a recorded KC cannot be matched to a model KC. So a model is compared with the flies on what does not depend on which unit is which.

**Three views of a population** are read, the same at every stage. They are the three rows of the figure above.

- **Strength by odor:** each odor's response rate (the fraction of units that respond to it) and each odor's mean response, both as multiples of their mean over the panel's odors.
- **Sharing between odors:** for each pair of odors, the cosine distance between their responses across the units, once on which units respond and once on how strongly the responders respond.
- **Breadth among units:** the distribution of the number of odors that a unit responds to.

Where the units are named glomeruli, at the ORNs and the PN dendrites, each glomerulus's response to each odor is read as a fourth view.

**The odors' strengths are re-ordered along the pathway.** aa is among the weakest odors at the PN dendrites, at {{E138/stages.csv:panel=megamat;condition=on;group=family_fitted;stage=pn_dendrite;row=common;odor=aa:rate_flies ~ .2f}} of the panel's mean response rate, and among the strongest at the KC somata, at {{E138/stages.csv:panel=megamat;condition=on;group=family_fitted;stage=kc_soma;row=common;odor=aa:rate_flies ~ .2f}}. B-cit goes the other way, from {{E138/stages.csv:panel=megamat;condition=on;group=family_fitted;stage=kc_claw;row=common;odor=B-cit:rate_flies ~ .2f}} at the claws to {{E138/stages.csv:panel=megamat;condition=on;group=family_fitted;stage=kc_soma;row=common;odor=B-cit:rate_flies ~ .2f}} at the somata. A model therefore cannot get the somata's strengths right by handing on the strengths of its input.

**The KC somata hold odors apart, and their cells respond to few odors.** At the ORNs nearly every glomerulus responds to many of the odors, and two odors share most of the glomeruli that respond. At the KC somata most cells respond to no odor or to one, and two odors share few of the cells that respond. The stages between lie between (the middle and bottom rows of the figure).

## How a model is compared with the flies {#target}

### One-fly units and the floor {#one-fly-units}

**One-fly units.** A view's error is the root mean squared difference between the model's view and the flies' mean view, over the view's entries: the odors, the pairs or the bins. It is divided by the same difference for one fly against the mean of the other flies. A value of 1 says that the model is as far from the flies as one fly is from the others, and lower is better ([how it is computed](#fn-one-fly)).

**The floor** is what a resampled set of the same flies gives against the measured set. No model can be expected below it. **The most atypical fly** is the fly furthest from the mean of the others, and it sets the line for "inside" ([below](#gating)).

**The room for a model is between the floor and one fly.** By construction one fly reads about 1. The floors are near a third at every stage, so a model at 1 has about two thirds of a one-fly unit left to gain.

### Like for like {#like-for-like}

Both sides are read the same way at every stage, and a stage's reading is fixed before any model is read on it.

- **The flies' responses** are counted in units of their own noise, with one response criterion per stage ([footnote](#fn-criteria)).
- **The model's units are sampled and pooled as the recording was made:** the glomeruli that the driver line labels, and {{json:results/E134_population_baseline/lock.json:readings.constants.CLAW_REGION_SIZE}} claws to a claw region.
- **The model's values pass through one monotone curve per stage,** set on the pooled values and never on anything about odors. An indicator's unknown curve is then neither counted as error nor used to hide one ([footnote](#fn-stage-curve)).
- **The model is given the flies' trial noise.**

Every stage has a second reading as well, with another curve, another pooling or raw spike counts. A stage whose verdict changes between the two is "not readable" and stays out of the comparison until a reading holds.

### The numbers that are compared {#numbers}

- **The population error** of a stage is the root mean square of its views' errors, each in one-fly units: one number per stage, panel and condition.
- **The megamat stage error** is the root mean square of the errors of four megamat stages: the PN dendrites, the PN boutons, the KC claws and the KC somata. The ORN stage is the model's input, so it is read and not counted.
- **The megamat stage error on strength by odor** takes each stage's strength error alone. It is the number that models are chosen and gated on, for the reason of the next subsection.

**Why breadth cannot order the models.** Once a model's values pass through the stage's curve, its response rate, and with it most of its breadth, is the flies'. What is left differs less between the variants than a resampled set of flies moves: the middle half of the variants' breadth errors spans {{E134_population_baseline/writeup_breadth.csv:reading=curve_noise;condition=on;measure=breadth:iqr ~ .2f}} with APL on, against a floor of {{E134_population_baseline/writeup_breadth.csv:reading=curve_noise;condition=on;measure=breadth:flies_floor ~ .2f}}.

**What sharing and breadth add beside it.** They are reported for every model at every stage, with the error over all three views. They say where a model is furthest from the flies, as leads. And they guard a gain: a change that lowers the strength error and raises another view's error by more than that error's spread over the wirings is not an improvement.

### Choosing and gating {#gating}

- **An improvement** lowers the megamat stage error on strength by odor, on wirings and flies that the change was not fitted to, by more than the wirings' spread. It raises no stage's error and no held-out panel's by more than that stage's own spread.
- **A model is read as a population of flies.** The best family exists on eight wirings, each read as one fly, and a model's value is the mean over its wirings. Fits use four wirings and half of each stage's flies, and are read on the four other wirings against the other half.
- **A stage is "inside"** if the model is no further from the flies' mean than the most atypical fly is from the other flies, on {{const:experiments/E134_population_baseline.py:INSIDE_MIN_WIRINGS ~ w}} of the eight wirings. It is "far" if it is above {{const:experiments/E134_population_baseline.py:FAR_ERROR ~ .0f}} on as many.
- **A view's, a stage's or a single odor's share of an error is a lead,** never a result. Near-ties are confirmed on held-out flies, wirings or panels.
- **Realism is a constraint, not a target.** The four realism bands hold for every model run on the APL16 panel ([footnote](#fn-realism-bands)), and every section names what would be hard to defend in a fly.

### Before the calyx the model is too even across odors {#before-calyx}

### The antennal lobe held to the recorded PN dendrites {#antennal-lobe-dendrites}

**The question was whether the megamat stage error is made before the calyx.** In the antennal lobe held to the recorded PN dendrites, the model's PN stage is held to the recorded dendrites in two ways. It is fitted to them, and the recorded dendrites themselves are put in its place ("imposed"). The family's calyx stays as it is, with both response rates held again.

**With the calyx unchanged, nothing carries past the dendrites.** With the recorded dendrites imposed, the dendrites come nearer the flies, and the boutons, the claws and the somata all move away, on every wiring. The fitted stages bring the boutons nearer and move the claws and the somata further away. With the family's calyx as it is, a PN stage nearer the flies' is a worse input to it. A weaker normalization fitted together with the calyx does carry, as far as the claws ([below](#stage-fit)).

**"Imposed" keeps the flies' arrangement and the model's rates.** On the {{json:results/E135_antennal_lobe_dendrites/lock.json:len(glomeruli)}} glomeruli that the recordings and the model's input share, each PN's rate is set from the flies' mean dendrite response, rank for rank on the model's own rates. The dendrites enter in units of the flies' own noise, and once more as the change in fluorescence over its baseline (dF/F).

**The question was whether the family comes nearer the flies once the stages are in its loss,** and which of its structural choices the stages name. Until then the family's parameters had been set by the APL effect on the APL16 panel alone. In the best family fitted to all megamat stages at once, the loss of a fit is the megamat stage error on strength by odor.

**The design: {{json:results/E136_stage_population_fit/measure_strength/classes.json:len(classes) ~ w}} fits and a control, each read where it was not fitted.**

- **Fitted on one half, read on the other.** A fit is made on half of the family's eight wirings, against half of each stage's flies. It is read on the other wirings against the other flies, and the unrefitted family is read there too. Against half of the flies every model reads higher than against all of them, so the values of this section are compared with each other and not with the stage table.
- **Both response rates are held** at every step of a fit: the megamat flies' response rate with APL on, and each wiring's own response rate with APL off. A fit searches what the two rates leave free.
- **Each fit tests one structural choice.** The family refitted searches two scales of the calyx: that of APL's inhibition of the PN boutons and that of the inhibition among KCs. The others repeat that fit with one change each: the antennal lobe's normalization searched as well, APL's inhibition of the claws as a gain in place of a subtraction, a per-claw threshold searched as well, or the inhibition among KCs taken out.
- **The antennal lobe enters through two global terms.** The PN stages fitted to the recorded dendrites handed on no stage ([above](#antennal-lobe-dendrites)). So the fit with the antennal lobe's normalization searched has the additive term and the scale of the model's own normalization in its search, and no term per glomerulus.
- **The control fit** is the family refitted to flies whose odor labels are permuted. Its fall says what the freedom of a fit gains where there is nothing to learn.
- **A fit "holds"** if its megamat stage error falls by more than {{const:experiments/E136_stage_population_fit.py:IMPROVE_SE ~ .0f}} standard errors over the wirings and by more than the control fit's fall, and if nothing else gets worse by more than the unrefitted family's spread over its eight wirings. That covers every megamat stage, on strength by odor and over all its views, and the KC somata of the held-out panels of the test: validation2, the control mixtures as uploaded, and the APL16 panel with APL on and with APL off. The kiwi mixtures are read beside the test ([caveats](#caveats)).

## What carries over from the APL16 work {#apl16-carryover}

The work before this target is on the page "APL16 Summary". It held models to the per-pair APL effect: for each pair of odors of the APL16 panel, the distance between their KC responses with APL on minus the distance with APL off. Only what bears on reading the stage target is repeated here.

**What the best family is.** It is the model of [the stage table](#best-models), with three mechanisms that the earlier work added.

- **An APL that weights KCs by their breadth:** a KC's weight from APL grows with how broadly the KC responds without APL.
- **APL's inhibition of the PN boutons,** beside its inhibition of the claws.
- **An inhibition among KCs,** weighted by the hemibrain's synapse counts between them, that stays when APL's output is blocked.

**How it was fitted.** Four parameters were fitted to the per-pair APL effect of the APL16 recordings, with both response rates held at the flies' ([the APL-effect fits](#fn-apl-effect)). ## Caveats {#caveats}

**The mixture sessions have a fixed order, so the kiwi mixtures gate nothing.** In a mixture session the components are given first, the mixtures after them, and in most flies the components once more.

- **Every presentation of an odor evokes less than the one before.** The components' second block evokes {{min:E137_heldout_readings/position_decline.csv:panel=kmix;units=noise:component_rate_later_over_first_block ~ .2f}} to {{max:E137_heldout_readings/position_decline.csv:panel=kmix;units=noise:component_rate_later_over_first_block ~ .2f}} of their first block's response rate in the kiwi flies (the median per fly).
- **The fall follows an odor's own repeats, not the time in the session.** In the megamat sessions, whose order is drawn anew for each block, an odor's response rate falls by a factor of {{min:E137_heldout_readings/component_fall/megamat_repeats.csv:fall_with=repeats:rate_factor_per_repeat ~ .2f}} to {{max:E137_heldout_readings/component_fall/megamat_repeats.csv:fall_with=repeats:rate_factor_per_repeat ~ .2f}} per repeat in three of the four flies, where as many trials of other odors cost a factor of {{min:E137_heldout_readings/component_fall/megamat_repeats.csv:fall_with=repeats:rate_factor_per_block_of_places ~ .2f}} to {{max:E137_heldout_readings/component_fall/megamat_repeats.csv:fall_with=repeats:rate_factor_per_block_of_places ~ .2f}}.
- **So no reading sets fresh mixtures against fresh components.** The mixtures come after trials of their own components, while the components' first block is fresh, and the model has no adaptation. Sessions with the mixtures first or interleaved would settle it, and they would be new recordings.

**What was decided for the mixture panels.**

- **The kiwi mixtures leave the test of whether a fit holds.** They are reported beside it, with the readings against the components' first block and against their second block as bounds. No fit is gated on them.
- **The control mixtures stay in the test,** read as uploaded, with the second-block reading beside. The family is inside on them under every reading, so their class does not turn on the choice.
- **Megamat and validation2 are not touched.** Their order is drawn anew for each block and fly, so the fall enters the flies' spread and not their mean. The order of the APL16 recordings is not in their processed tables.

**Validation2 has been read once, for a regression.** The test of a common cause behind the odors' strength errors was judged on validation2's odors ([above](#strength-entries)). No class of glomeruli passed, so nothing is built on its result. Still, validation2 is no longer an unseen panel for anything chosen from those classes or from those readings: a change proposed from them has to be checked on other held-out odors or flies. For fits that take nothing from that test, validation2 stays held out.

**Further limits of the readings.**

- **The flies are few after the dendrites:** {{E134_population_baseline/writeup_stage_table.csv:group=megamat_fitted;stage=pn_bouton:n_flies ~ w}} bouton flies, {{E134_population_baseline/writeup_stage_table.csv:group=megamat_fitted;stage=kc_claw:n_flies ~ w}} claw flies and {{E134_population_baseline/writeup_stage_table.csv:group=megamat_fitted;stage=kc_soma:n_flies ~ w}} soma flies, so a half of a stage's flies is two flies. The megamat soma flies respond at two levels, from {{E134_population_baseline/writeup_soma_criteria.csv:cohort=megamat, Remy's four flies:rate_min ~ .3f}} to {{E134_population_baseline/writeup_soma_criteria.csv:cohort=megamat, Remy's four flies:rate_max ~ .3f}}, which shows on sharing and breadth and not on strength by odor.
- **"Inside" is a loose line.** It is measured against the most atypical fly, and a model that is inside can still be most of a one-fly unit above the floor.
- **The measure's consistency rests on few flies after the boutons.** Among saved variants it is shown at one stage of one panel, the KC somata of the APL16 panel. At the megamat stages it was read over the evaluations of the best family fitted to all megamat stages at once ([above](#stage-fit)). There the megamat stage error passes as a whole, carried by the dendrites and the boutons. The claws and the somata, with two flies to a half, do not order the evaluations alike against the two halves.
- **The equal weights are a convention.** The megamat stage error weighs its four stages alike, and a population error its views. Every stage's and every view's error is given beside a combined number for that reason.
- **The stages are different flies, indicators and compartments.** The PN-dendrite recordings hold {{json:results/E135_antennal_lobe_dendrites/lock.json:len(glomeruli)}} of the model's {{json:results/E135_antennal_lobe_dendrites/timing_step1.json:size.n_input}} input glomeruli, and the bouton and claw regions carry no labels.
- **The family was fitted to the APL16 panel.** That panel is held out from fits to the megamat stages, and not for the family as it stands. Twelve of its odors are megamat odors.
- **One mapped brain.** Seven of the family's eight wirings are shuffles of the hemibrain's, and the range over the eight is not the range between flies.
- **Concentrations.** Every number of the APL16 panel involves paa, euc and -aPine ([footnote](#fn-concentrations)).

## Experiments running or not yet run, and what each would tell us {#pending}

Pending work is judged on the megamat stage error on strength by odor, on wirings and flies that a change was not fitted to, with every stage's error and the other views beside it. - **The weaker antennal-lobe normalization without the calyx refit, along its strength (running).** The one fit of the megamat stages that holds joins three changes: a much larger additive term of the PN normalization, and changed scales of APL's inhibition of the PN boutons and of the inhibition among KCs ([above](#stage-fit)). This experiment reads them apart with nothing fitted and both response rates held. It runs the family's own calyx at four strengths of the normalization, and each of the fit's two calyx scales by itself, on the megamat, the held-out and the APL16 inputs. Its first step, with no run, reads the recordings: each odor's response at the PN dendrites against its summed ORN input, beside the model's PN stage at each strength.
  - **If a strength holds with the family's own calyx and at no price on the APL effect,** one global parameter is changed and nothing in the calyx. That model is put to those who direct the project as the base for later fits.
  - **If a strength holds only at a price,** the result is the table of what each strength gives the megamat stages and takes from the APL effect, and the choice is theirs.

  - **What the recordings show bears on each of these.** If the flies' dendrites show a normalization as weak as the fit's, the fitted term has a reading in the fly. If they show the family's stronger one, the term stands in for something else that spreads the odors' strengths before the calyx.

Whether either adds once the normalization is the fitted one is not known. If one adds on wirings and flies that it was not fitted to, the stages name a second structural choice. - **The reading of the mixture sessions (open).** Against the components' second block the fitted model is further from the flies than the family, and against their first block it is not. If the second-block reading were taken into the test, the fitted model's standing on the held-out panels would have to be read again ([caveats](#caveats)).
- **aa between the claws and the somata (read with no simulation; what remains open).** In the flies aa rises from the claws to the somata above what its input predicts, through KCs that respond to few odors. No saved run of the model does this ([above](#claw-soma-aa)).

If it raises aa, the megamat stage error has room down to the error without aa's entry. If none does, the cause lies in aa's input.

  - **aa's input through the glomeruli fed by ORNs with ionotropic receptors, raised (held for a decision of those who direct the project).** It sets an input factor by the KC recordings. ### How one-fly units and the floor are computed {#fn-one-fly}

Each view has one or two parts. Strength by odor has the odors' response rates and the odors' mean responses. Sharing between odors has the cosine distance on which units respond and on how strongly the responders respond. Breadth among units has one part, and so has the glomerulus view.

- **A part's one-fly unit** is the root mean squared distance of one fly's part from the mean part of the other flies, over the flies and the part's entries.
- **A part's error** for a model is the root mean squared difference between the model's part and the flies' mean part, over the entries, divided by that unit.
- **A view's error** is the root mean square of its parts' errors, and a population error is the root mean square of its views' errors.
- **The floor** is the mean error of {{const:kcq/population_fit.py:N_BOOT ~ ,d}} sets of the same flies, drawn with replacement, each scored as a model is against the measured set.
- **One fly against the others** is each fly's error against the mean of the other flies, in the units of all the flies.

A unit from four flies is itself uncertain. In the best family fitted to all megamat stages at once ([above](#stage-fit)) a half of a stage's flies can be two flies. There the one-fly units of all the stage's flies are kept, since two flies give one difference as their unit.

### The monotone curve per stage, and the two readings of a stage {#fn-stage-curve}

A calcium indicator's signal is some rising function of a unit's activity, and that function is not known. So a model's values at a stage are not compared with the flies' as they are.

- **The curve.** The model's pooled values, over every unit and odor of the stage, are mapped onto the flies' pooled values rank for rank, at {{json:results/E134_population_baseline/lock.json:readings.constants.N_CURVE_KNOTS}} evenly spaced quantiles. The curve is non-decreasing and is set for each wiring, with APL on.
- **What it cannot do.** It is set on two pooled distributions, with no odor, unit or pair label. A permutation of either side gives the same curve, so it cannot move an odor's strength relative to another's except through the values themselves.
- **The noise.** After the curve the model's values get the flies' trial noise: the spread of single trials about their odor means, averaged over as many trials as the flies were given.
- **The criterion.** A model unit then responds if its value is above the flies' criterion of the stage.

Every stage has a second reading fixed beforehand: a straight line in place of the curve, another pooling of the boutons or the claws, or at the KC somata raw spike counts with two or more spikes as a response. A stage whose class differs between its two readings is "not readable".

### The response criterion at each stage {#fn-criteria}

A response of the flies is counted in units of noise, so that an indicator's scale does not enter.

- **At the ORNs and the PN dendrites** the unit is the spread of single trials about their odor means, and a glomerulus responds above {{E134_population_baseline/flies_views.csv:population=orn__megamat__wild_type;name=strength:criterion ~ .0f}} of those units.
- **At the boutons and the claws** the unit is the noise before the odor, the standard deviation of the region's signal within the pre-odor frames, and a region responds above {{E134_population_baseline/flies_views.csv:population=pn_bouton__megamat__wild_type;name=strength:criterion ~ .0f}} of those units.
- **At the KC somata** the unit is the same, and the level is set per set of recordings. For the APL16 panel it is {{E134_population_baseline/writeup_soma_criteria.csv:cohort=apl16, control flies:criterion ~ .2f}}, the level at which the control flies keep the response rate of their experimenter's own criterion, {{E134_population_baseline/writeup_soma_criteria.csv:cohort=apl16, control flies:rate_mean ~ .3f}}.
- **The megamat and validation2 somata** take {{E134_population_baseline/writeup_soma_criteria.csv:cohort=megamat, Remy's four flies:criterion ~ .2f}}, the level at which the megamat soma flies keep the response rate of their experimenter's own criterion, {{E134_population_baseline/writeup_soma_criteria.csv:cohort=megamat, Remy's four flies:rate_mean ~ .4f}}. At the APL16 panel's level they would respond at {{E134_population_baseline/writeup_soma_criteria.csv:cohort=megamat, Remy's four flies:rate_mean_at_apl16_criterion ~ .3f}}.
- **The mixture somata as uploaded** are in each cell's z-score over its session, with a level set the same way. The readings by position in the session are in baseline-noise units.

A model's KC responds on raw spike counts if it fires two or more spikes to the odor.

### The realism bands {#fn-realism-bands}

A model run on the APL16 panel is flagged if it leaves one of four ranges. The model's side is counted on raw spike counts, two or more spikes as a response, and the flies' side in baseline-noise units.

- **The response rate with APL on** is between {{const:kcq/apl_effect.py:RATE_ON_BAND.0 ~ .3f}} and {{const:kcq/apl_effect.py:RATE_ON_BAND.1 ~ .3f}}. The control flies' is {{data:noise_ctrl_frac ~ .3f}}.
- **The ratio of the response rates with APL off and with APL on** is between {{const:kcq/apl_effect.py:RATE_RATIO_BAND.0 ~ .1f}} and {{const:kcq/apl_effect.py:RATE_RATIO_BAND.1 ~ .1f}}. The flies' is {{data:noise_rate_ratio_fraction ~ .2f}}.
- **The response rate ρ with APL on,** the correlation over odors between the model's and the flies' response rates, is at least {{const:kcq/apl_effect.py:RATE_RHO_MIN ~ .2f}}.
- **The distribution of the number of odors per KC** is no further from the flies' mean distribution than the most atypical recording is from the others: {{data:noise_ctrl_odors_per_kc_tv_max ~ .3f}} with APL on and {{data:noise_tnt_odors_per_kc_tv_max ~ .3f}} with APL off, in total variation distance.

The bands are reported and never optimized. On megamat the overall response rate is an operating point: it is held at the megamat flies' value and reported beside it.

### The APL-effect statistics {#fn-apl-effect}

For each of the 120 odor pairs of the APL16 panel, the per-pair APL effect is the distance between the two odors' KC responses with APL on minus their distance with APL off. In the flies that is the control flies' distance minus the distance with APL output blocked (TNT), each the mean over the recordings of the cosine distance. In a model it is taken from one run with APL on and one with APL's output weights at zero.

- **The APL-effect loss** is the root mean squared difference between the model's and the flies' per-pair effect. Its floor is the loss of a resampled set of the recordings against the measured set.
- **The mean APL effect** is the mean over the pairs, and **the pattern part** is the loss with both means removed.
- **The pattern part splits exactly** into an amplitude part, how much wider or narrower the model spreads its effect over the pairs than the flies do, and a shape part, which is zero when the model's effect is the flies' stretched about its mean.
- **The two correlations** over the pairs, ρ and Pearson r, are descriptions. No model is chosen by a correlation: neither sees the mean or the spread of the effect.

On this page the statistics are read through the calibrated readout, a saturating function of a model KC's spike count whose half-saturation is set on the model's own counts with APL on. The fits that made the family, the APL-effect fits and the APL-effect fit on re-drawn wirings, and every other reading of these statistics are on the page "APL16 Summary".

### Odor concentrations in each dataset {#fn-concentrations}

The megamat panel is at 10^-3 at every stage, in the recordings and in the model's input. The mixtures' ORN input is the recorded response to each mixture, not a sum or a maximum over its components.

The 16 odors of the APL16 panel and the ORN recordings that stand in for them in the model (log10 of the dilution by volume). Twelve odors are at the megamat panel's concentration. For the four odors shared with validation2, the KC recordings used other concentrations than the ORN recordings of that panel.

| odor | KC recordings of the APL16 panel | ORN recordings used | ORN panel | difference (ORN minus KC, log10 units) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2h, IaA, pa, 2-but, eb, ep, 6al, t2h, 1-8ol, 1-5ol, 1-6ol, ms | -3 | -3 | megamat | 0 |
| paa | -3 | -4 | validation2 | -1 (ORN weaker) |
| -aPine | -2 | -1.5 | validation2 | +0.5 (ORN stronger) |
| euc | -2 | -1.75 | validation2 | +0.25 (ORN stronger) |
| B-myr | -2 | -2 | validation2 | 0 |

**The substitution rule.** Where no ORN recording exists at the concentration that the KCs were given, a recording within one log10 unit, inclusive, stands in. Every result that involves the substituted odors' input is then approximate: here everything of the APL16 panel that involves paa, euc or -aPine. The validation2 panel is read at matched concentrations on both sides.
