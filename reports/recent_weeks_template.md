re-drawn: Of the PN→KC wiring: a randomized version of the hemibrain wiring that the model otherwise uses, made either by permuting the claws among the KCs of each type or by a shuffle that keeps every glomerulus pair's count of shared KCs. A model on a re-drawn wiring stands for another fly with the same statistics.
best family: The family of models that stands best on the main target: APL inhibition weighted by how broadly each KC is driven, KC–KC inhibition, no per-claw threshold and no glomerulus gain, fitted to the APL effect at the control flies' response rate. It is read on eight wirings, the real one and seven randomized versions of it, each at its own fit.
APL effect: Of one odor pair, its odor-odor distance with APL on minus its distance with APL off. In the KC data, the control flies' distance minus the distance with APL output blocked (TNT), each the mean over recordings of the cosine distance; in a model, its distance with APL on minus its distance with APL acutely off.
APL-effect loss: The root mean squared difference between a model's and the KC data's per-pair APL effect over the 120 odor pairs; lower is better. It is the main target. It splits exactly into a mean part and a pattern part, and its floor is the loss that a second sample of the same flies would show against the first.
pattern part: The APL-effect loss with both means removed: how far the model's pattern of the APL effect across the 120 pairs is from the flies'. Since 2026-11-13 it is the measure on which variants are compared. It splits exactly into an amplitude part (the model spreads its effect over the pairs more or less widely than the flies) and a shape part.
calibrated readout: Each model spike count n replaced by n / (n + k) before the distances are taken, with k set on the model's counts with APL on. It compresses large counts as a calcium indicator does and leaves which KCs respond unchanged.
realism bands: The four ranges that a model has to stay inside for its loss to count: its response rate with APL on, the ratio of its response rates with APL off and on, its response rate ρ with APL on, and its distribution of the number of odors each KC responds to, each set from the recordings.
common error: The part of the best family's pattern error that every wiring shares: its APL effect is too small among 2h, IaA, pa, eb, ep and ms and too large among 1-8ol, 1-5ol, 1-6ol and 6al.
wirings of the earlier searches: The real wiring and three randomized ones, on which a change of the best family is searched, chosen or fitted first.
held-out wirings: The best family's other four wirings, on which a change is read after it has been searched, chosen or fitted on the first four, against the half of the recordings that the choice did not use.
margin of an equal: The band inside which a changed model counts as level with the unchanged fits: a pattern part within 0.003 of theirs against all recordings and against halves of the recordings, inside the realism bands. Every test fixed its margins before any run.
keeps the gain: Of a replacement for the best family's KC–KC inhibition: its pattern part is at or below the half-way point between the family's and that of the family with no KC–KC inhibition, on the first four wirings and on the other four.
oracle: A model manipulation that is handed part of the answer, here an input whose entries were searched against the KC recordings. A ceiling test, never a mechanism.
read in proportion: A model's APL response taken as it is, as if the calcium indicator reported APL's activity in proportion to it.
quality caveat: The experimenter has released these recordings for use and has not checked their quality. No recording was left out on quality, and every result that rests on them carries this.
on hold: Not to be used until cleared: nothing new is read from the material, and no number, figure or verdict that rests on it is shown.

## In brief {#brief}

- **The main target did not move.** The best family's pattern part stands at {{E72/population.csv:group=all:cal_pattern ~ .3f}} through the calibrated readout, as a mean over eight wirings, for a floor of {{data:apl_effect_pattern_floor ~ .3f}} (lower is better). Its mean APL effect is {{E72/population.csv:group=all:cal_mean ~ +.3f}} for the flies' {{data:apl_effect_mean ~ +.3f}}. Every change of the model in the period was level with the family or cost it something. No base model, bound or reference was changed.
- **The common error is not the input's and not the wiring's.** Fitted inputs, the spread between ORN flies, the glomeruli that the input leaves at zero and a second brain's proportions of claws were all read. None carries it ([the input and the wiring](#input-wiring)).
- **APL's side of the family is described, and closed as the source of the error.** For a KC the family's APL is close to a fixed raise of its threshold, most of it made at the PN boutons. No other weighting, summation, gain or static form does better ([the family's APL](#apl-form)). Held against recordings of the flies' APL, the family's APL is flatter along concentration ([the flies' APL](#flies-apl)).
- **Below the somata the model's PN boutons are too even across odors.** No transfer of the PN stage that was tried repairs that, and the one remedy with an effect costs the pattern part ([the stages](#stages)).
- **The gap in ρ on odor-odor distances sits in the input of paa and B-myr.** Three fixed changes of that input raise both ρ's where nothing was fitted. They were not adopted, and the input route is closed ([the gap in ρ](#rho-gap)).
- **The family needs a second inhibition among its KCs, and a part of it has to outlast the block of APL's output.** It need not be the hemibrain's KC-to-KC partners, and a neuron that pools KCs can carry half of it. A static level cannot ([the inhibition among KCs](#kckc)).
- **Open on 2026-11-23.** Two experiments are running, and several choices wait on a decision ([what is running](#running)).

## The common error: the input and the wiring {#input-wiring}

## Decisions taken in the period {#decisions}

Each was taken by Tom on the date given.

- **2026-11-13, how the main target is read.** Variants are compared on the pattern part of the APL-effect loss, given with its amplitude part and its shape part. No variant is chosen by a correlation, the pairs are not weighted by their reliability, and per-odor readings of the error are leads. These were Yang's recommendations.
- **2026-11-15, what may be read.** Connectome tables derived in separate work are on hold. A recording that exists only as a raw movie is not read. The further recordings that Yang has processed may be used, with the quality caveat.
- **2026-11-17, the connectome.** Two connectome data sets are used and no other: the hemibrain and the male CNS, both as curated by Pratyush.
- **2026-11-17, a second aim.** ρ on odor-odor distances is to be raised without a cost on the mean APL effect or on any other measure of the match ([above](#rho-gap)).
- **2026-11-19, three statistics are reported only.** The fruity × small-ester block, the natural-source alignment and the structure against wild-type somata are still computed and shown. No loss term, test or verdict rests on them.
- **2026-11-19, no new wet-lab experiments.** A result that ends in "a recording would test this" is a prediction to report, and nothing is planned around it.
- **2026-11-19, the input searches.** The fall of the response rate ρ under a searched input is not counted as a real cost. Even so, no further search of many free input factors is planned. Changes with few parameters, or with a basis in anatomy or physiology, come first.
- **2026-11-20, the model's code.** New defaults and changes of the model's code are Yang's to approve for now. By default, a claw with synapses from two boutons of its PN now belongs to the bouton that gives it most of them.
- **2026-11-21, the three entries are not adopted.** The lab's recordings stay the model's input for paa and B-myr ([above](#rho-gap)).
- **2026-11-22, no glomerulus labels for the bouton recordings.** They cannot be had from the boutons' positions, and labels inferred from tuning would be circular.
